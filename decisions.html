DECISION ROOM

Don't rush the decision.

Good decisions don't always come from having more information. Sometimes they come from separating facts, emotions, values and consequences.

1. Define the decision

Write the decision as clearly as possible.

2. What are your options?

Keep the options simple.

3. Pros & Cons

Think about the consequences of each option.

Option A — Advantages

Option A — Disadvantages

Option B — Advantages

Option B — Disadvantages

4. Fear or fact?

Some decisions feel dangerous because our mind predicts a future. Separate what you know from what you're imagining.

5. Three futures

Imagine the same option under three different outcomes.

6. Values check

Which values matter most in this decision?

7. Future-self test

Imagine yourself one year from now.

8. Regret test

Which regret would be harder to live with?

9. Opportunity cost

Choosing one path usually means giving something else up.

10. Final reflection

Don't ask "What should I do?" Ask better questions.
